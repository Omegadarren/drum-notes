Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Scavenger Hunters ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: If you create an account, we collect your display name and email address.
  • Photos: When you photograph items during a hunt, those images are uploaded to our secure cloud storage to verify completion and display on leaderboards.

Information collected automatically

  • Location data: We collect your GPS location while you are actively participating in a hunt to verify you are within the designated hunt boundary. Location is only accessed while the app is in use; we do not track location in the background.
  • Usage data: Hunt scores, completion times, and gameplay statistics are stored to power leaderboards and your personal profile.
  • Device identifiers: A unique anonymous identifier is assigned to your device session to enable gameplay before you create an account.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the app and its features
  • To verify hunt item completion using AI image analysis (OpenAI GPT-4o-mini)
  • To display leaderboards and player statistics
  • To send push notifications about hunt activity (only if you grant permission)
  • To provide customer support

3. AI Photo Analysis

Photos you submit as proof of found items are sent to OpenAI's API for automated verification. These images are processed transiently and are not stored by OpenAI for training purposes. OpenAI's privacy policy applies to this processing: openai.com/privacy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Google Firebase: Our backend infrastructure provider (authentication, database, file storage). Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI: For AI photo verification as described above.
  • Other players: Your display name and leaderboard scores are visible to other users of the app.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data and hunt history for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

6. Children's Privacy

Scavenger Hunters is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), Firebase Security Rules to restrict data access, and authentication required for all data operations. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request data deletion, please contact us at:
